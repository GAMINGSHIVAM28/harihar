Our Story & Journey

Harihar Pharma Distributors is a dedicated wholesale medicine supplier based in Bilaspur, Chhattisgarh. Established in 2018, we have built our reputation on providing genuine pharmaceutical products at competitive wholesale prices to pharmacies, clinics, and healthcare businesses across the region.

Our mission is to make quality medicines accessible through reliable wholesale channels, ensuring that pharmacies and healthcare facilities can serve their customers with authentic products at fair prices.

Quality Standards

Authenticity & WHO-GMP Compliance

At Harihar Pharma, we strictly enforce drug quality standards. All medicines are sourced directly from leading manufacturers (including Sun Pharma, Cipla, Alkem, Abbott, Dr. Reddy's) and certified WHO-GMP laboratories.

We maintain strict batch-tracking protocols, ensuring that GST invoicing, manufacturing dates, and clinical batch numbers match manufacturer verification codes perfectly. This prevents counterfeit or short-expiry drugs from entering the retail supply chain.
